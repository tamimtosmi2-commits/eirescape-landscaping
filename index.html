The Irish Climate Difference

Why Standard Landscaping Fails in Ireland

Ireland receives an average of 1,200mm of rainfall annually, with clay-heavy soils that quickly become compacted, saturated, and waterlogged.

01

Permeable Sub-Base Engineering

We dig deeper (minimum 150mm MOT Type 1 aggregate) to ensure water drains freely under paving, preventing frost heave and winter slab cracks.

02

Certified Non-Slip Porcelain

Standard polished sandstone grows green algae and becomes dangerously slippery when damp. Our vitrified porcelain has a certified anti-slip rating of R11.

03

Protected Microclimate Sanctuaries

We strategically position cedar lath windbreaks and louvered pergolas so you can enjoy your garden even during typical Irish drizzle.

SuDS

Sustainable Drainage Standards

Every patio slope and soakaway we engineer is designed to adhere to Irish environmental drainage regulations.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need planning permission for a patio or pergola in Ireland?

Under Irish planning regulations, ground-level patios and open-sided garden pergolas under 4 meters in height generally do not require planning permission, provided they fall within standard residential exemptions. Our team handles compliance checks during the design phase.

How does your 5-year structural guarantee work?

Our guarantee covers all hard landscaping foundations, structural retaining walls, porcelain pointing, and drainage systems against subsidence, cracking, or installation failure. We build to strict ALCI standards to ensure long-term durability.

When is the best time of year to landscape an Irish garden?

Hard landscaping (paving, pergolas, retaining walls) can be constructed year-round in Ireland. Spring and early autumn are prime for turf laying and perennial planting so roots establish ahead of summer growth.

How long does a typical garden transformation take?

A standard suburban patio and lawn project takes between 7 to 10 working days. Full luxury renovations with pergolas and custom masonry generally take 2 to 3 weeks. We provide a firm schedule before breaking ground.
